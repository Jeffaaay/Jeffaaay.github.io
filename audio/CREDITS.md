## Previous recording collection / historical processing

Downloaded and license-checked 2026-10-10 from the primary creator submission pages below. These are free licensed review candidates. The authors do not endorse this portfolio. Runtime media requests are local; no account, paid service, API key or dependency was added. All downloaded audio files are preserved byte-for-byte under descriptive filenames. The engine applies playback gain/fades, mixes the three loops, overlaps 650 ms of music and 350 ms of ambience at the loop boundary, and varies footstep playback rate (1.06 / 1.12). In-world footfalls use at most 170 ms of each clip, with a 2 ms attack ramp and a 25 ms fade before a scheduled stop; a peak-relative onset scan preserves an 8 ms lead-in on long clips. Short concrete clips keep their complete source attack. This truncation applies only to footsteps, not music or UI cues. These runtime adaptations are made for this portfolio.
