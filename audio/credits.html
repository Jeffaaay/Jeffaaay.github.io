Stream Sounds (stream2.ogg) by kurt, 2011. Creator and source · CC BY 3.0. In-world adaptation: gain, fades and 350 ms loop overlap. Original download preserved.

Foot contacts / grass

Three alternating Foley variations from Impact Sounds by Kenney. Creator and source · CC0 1.0. General footsteps, adapted in-world at lower gain and slightly faster playback for the dog. Long tails are shortened to at most 170 ms with an onset-aware start and a 25 ms fade. These previews retain the original files.

Foot contacts / concrete

Two alternating Foley variations from Impact Sounds by Kenney. Creator and source · CC0 1.0. Used near the concrete bridge with adjusted volume and playback rate, preserving the short source attack and fading the final tail. These previews retain the original files.
